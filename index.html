Signal conditioning, data acquisition and data reduction for industrial monitoring

Roseate Technology LLC designs hardware and firmware that condition sensor signals, reduce them to meaningful measurements, and publish the results to SCADA, PLC and other supervisory control systems.

Get in touch

Capabilities

Signal conditioning

Analog front ends for industrial sensors: excitation, amplification, filtering and precision conversion for clean, high-bandwidth measurements.

Data reduction

On-device post-processing that takes signals sampled in the kilohertz range and reduces and combines them into the values that matter, down to a single pass or fail health signal. Built-in RMS, spectra and envelope analysis, plus reductions your own engineers define and run in the node. Or stream signals to your supervisory system at up to full bandwidth, limited only by the transport, including UDP push to any listener on the network.

Presentation

A built-in web interface for commissioning and diagnostics, with live views of the signal at every stage of processing.

Publishing to supervisory systems

Real-time live data over EtherCAT® and PROFINET® for use by your real-time supervisory systems, plus Modbus® TCP and OPC UA® for SCADA, PLC and plant historian systems. All data is time-synchronized and can be timestamped from IEEE 1588 PTP or a plant or lab reference clock, so measurements line up across every node and system.

Packaging

DIN-rail modules for the control cabinet first, then sealed, IP-rated housings for mounting at the machine, and bespoke mechanical packaging designed in-house to your requirements.
