Capabilities

Signal conditioning

Analog front ends for industrial sensors: excitation, amplification, filtering and precision conversion for clean, high-bandwidth measurements.

EtherCAT subdevices

Deterministic EtherCAT interfaces that put conditioned measurements and outputs onto the network with tight timing.

Compact embedded I/O

Small-form-factor boards, including mikroBUS add-on boards and BeagleBone capes, for prototyping and integration.

Embedded firmware

Real-time firmware for microcontroller platforms, built alongside the hardware for data acquisition and control applications.

About

Roseate Technology is a small engineering company based in Minnesota. We focus on practical, well-documented hardware for engineers who need dependable measurements and real-time I/O in test, monitoring and industrial automation systems.
